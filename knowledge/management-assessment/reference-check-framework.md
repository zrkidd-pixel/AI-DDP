# Management Assessment / Reference-Check Framework

Referenced by: Management Assessment Agent, Thesis / IC Memo Agent (for the
management-risk section)

## What this workstream is actually answering

Not "is the CEO impressive in a meeting," but: **will this leadership team
execute the specific value-creation plan this fund is underwriting, and what
happens if a key individual leaves during the hold period?** These are
different questions and require different evidence.

## Assessment dimensions

- **Track record specificity.** Prior results attributed to this individual
  should be checkable against a specific role, timeframe, and outcome — not a
  generic "grew the business" claim. Distinguish results driven by the
  individual from results driven by market tailwinds during their tenure.
- **Fit to the specific plan, not general competence.** A management team that
  built a business through organic growth may or may not be the right team to
  execute an acquisitive roll-up strategy, and vice versa — assess fit to
  *this* thesis's value-creation levers, not leadership ability in the
  abstract.
- **Depth beyond the top individual.** Single-person dependency (the business
  effectively *is* the founder) is a distinct risk from a thin bench below a
  competent CEO. Map out who actually holds institutional knowledge and
  customer relationships beyond the top one or two names.
- **Succession and retention risk.** What triggers departure risk around a
  change of control (vesting cliffs, culture shift, reduced autonomy under new
  ownership), and what's the actual replacement difficulty if a key person
  leaves in the first 12–24 months post-close?

## Reference-check structure

- Prefer references who worked *with* the individual in a peer or reporting
  relationship over references the individual selected themselves, where
  possible — selected references are a biased sample by construction.
- Ask for specific examples, not general characterizations ("tell me about a
  time X had to manage a underperforming quarter" beats "is X a good leader").
- Cross-check claims made in management presentations against what references
  independently describe — discrepancies are diligence findings, not just
  awkward moments.

## What this agent should hand off, not conclude on its own

Personnel decisions and incentive-alignment offers are ultimately human,
often partner-level, judgment calls. This agent's output is structured
findings and flagged risks — not a hire/replace recommendation, which belongs
in the Thesis gate's human-owned judgment-call list, not asserted as a
conclusion.
