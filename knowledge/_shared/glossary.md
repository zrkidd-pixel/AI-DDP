# Glossary

Referenced by: all agents. Shared vocabulary so "leverage" or "EV" means the
same thing in every phase's output.

- **EV (Enterprise Value):** Market equity + total debt − cash & equivalents.
  Always state whether an EV figure is "current" (today's price, before any
  premium) or "transaction" (post-premium, the value implied by the deal).
- **Market equity:** Share price × shares outstanding, at a stated date. State
  whether shares outstanding is basic, weighted-average, or fully diluted —
  they are not interchangeable.
- **Net debt:** Total debt (short + long-term) minus cash & equivalents. Can be
  negative (net cash position).
- **Transaction EV:** Enterprise value inclusive of the acquisition premium —
  the value the buyer is actually paying for, not the pre-announcement market
  value.
- **Equity check:** The sponsor equity required to close the transaction —
  transaction EV plus all fees, minus new debt raised.
- **EV/EBITDA:** The core valuation multiple used throughout this framework.
  State explicitly whether the EV in the numerator is current or transaction
  EV — these produce very different multiples and are not interchangeable.
- **LTM (Last Twelve Months):** Trailing twelve-month financials as of a stated
  date — not the same as "most recent complete fiscal year" unless the two
  happen to coincide. State which one is being used.
- **MOIC (Multiple on Invested Capital):** Total value returned divided by
  capital invested. A return-multiple metric, distinct from IRR (which is
  time-weighted).
- **IRR (Internal Rate of Return):** The annualized return that sets the net
  present value of all cash flows to zero. Sensitive to timing, not just
  magnitude, of cash flows — a fast exit at a lower MOIC can have a higher IRR
  than a slow exit at a higher MOIC.
- **Pro forma leverage:** New debt raised in the transaction divided by
  EBITDA — the leverage the business will carry immediately post-close.
- **Current net leverage:** The target's *existing* net debt divided by
  EBITDA, before any transaction — a measure of how levered the business
  already is going into a deal.
- **QoE (Quality of Earnings):** The diligence workstream that adjusts reported
  EBITDA to a normalized, run-rate figure — see
  `financial-diligence/qoe-redflag-checklist.md`.
- **MNPI (Material Non-Public Information):** See
  `compliance-mnpi/mnpi-framework.md` for the full test.
- **Rollover equity:** Equity the seller (often management) reinvests into the
  post-close capital structure rather than cashing out entirely — distinct
  from new sponsor equity.
