# IC Memo Template

Referenced by: Thesis / IC Memo Agent

**This is a generic structure — replace it with your specific firm's actual IC
memo template and house style before real use.** The sections below are the
substance every version needs; the format (page count, headers, exhibits) is
firm-specific and should not be copied from a training example.

## Standard sections

1. **Executive summary / recommendation.** The ask, the headline economics
   (entry multiple, target returns), and the recommendation in a few
   sentences — a reader should understand the decision being requested
   without reading further, even if they then do.
2. **Sector investment thesis.** Why this sector, growth drivers, risks and
   mitigants, consolidation opportunity — sourced per
   `_shared/citation-standards.md`.
3. **Market overview.** Size, growth, structure/fragmentation, regulatory
   environment, key success factors — see
   `market-intelligence/market-sizing-methodology.md`.
4. **Comparable company / target analysis.** The screened universe, with
   required financial and sector-specific metrics per company.
5. **Diligence summary.** Financial (QoE-adjusted EBITDA and why it differs
   from reported), commercial (concentration, competitive position),
   management assessment findings — each with its own tagged sourcing, not
   folded into the narrative without attribution.
6. **Transaction structure and returns.** Equity check, financing structure,
   base/upside/downside case per
   `thesis-icmemo/returns-sensitivity-conventions.md`.
7. **Value creation plan.** The specific levers and 100-day plan highlights —
   not a restatement of the generic taxonomy, but this deal's specific plan.
8. **Risk section (mandatory, not optional).** PE ownership risk, industry
   risk, company risk, management risk — populated with real, specific risks
   for this deal, not boilerplate. A memo with a thin or missing risk section
   should fail the Thesis gate regardless of how strong the rest of the
   analysis is.
9. **Judgment calls for the human/IC to explicitly own.** Anything that was a
   close call, a deviation from clean criteria, or a decision requiring
   institutional risk appetite rather than analysis — surfaced explicitly, not
   buried in the narrative.

## Gate check before this memo is considered done

- Every substantive claim tagged per `_shared/citation-standards.md`.
- Risk section present and specific to this deal.
- Recommendation and headline economics stated plainly in the executive
  summary, not only derivable from reading the full memo.
- Format matches the firm's actual template, not a generic default.
